CROSSBEAM QUICK GUIDE
=====================

TO USE (learners)
  Double-click index.html (Chrome or Edge). No internet or install needed.
  Leave it open next to the instrument software:
    - press  /  to search ("tilt", "outgas", "probe", "EHT", "vent" ...)
    - the pink chips under the title jump straight to the most-asked items
    - click any picture or clip to enlarge it
    - click the numbered circle on a step to tick it off

TO ADD OR EDIT CONTENT (author)
  1. Open index.html and click "Author" (top right), or open index.html#author
  2. Click "Connect the guide folder" once and pick THIS folder.
     Every change you make is then saved automatically into this folder:
        content.js   text, steps, highlight positions
        clips/       recorded video clips
        img/         pictures
  3. On any step:
        "Record clip"  -> Share screen -> Record -> do the task -> Stop
                          (Chrome's "Stop sharing" bar also stops it)
        "Add picture / video file" -> use a file you already have
  4. The highlight editor opens after every clip:
        Click   click where the cursor is at that moment: pink halo + click ripple
        Move    same, without the ripple
        Trace   plays at half speed - follow the cursor in the video with your mouse
        Box/Ring  highlight a control (optionally with a label)
        Crop    zoom learners into just the part of the screen that matters
        Trim    cut dead time from the start / end
  5. "Edit text" changes a step; "Draft - verify" badges mark content that still
     needs checking against the real instrument. Switch the badge off in "Edit text".

SHARING
  Copy the whole folder (index.html, content.js, img/, clips/) to the instrument PC or a
  shared drive. Keep the folder structure - clips and pictures are loaded by relative path.

NOTES
  - Clips are WebM video (loop silently, like a GIF but sharper and smaller).
  - If the browser cannot write to a folder, "Export..." in the author bar downloads
    content.js and the clips so you can copy them in by hand.
  - The FIB-user login from the V1 guide is shown in "FIB preparation"; remove it in
    Author mode if the guide will be shared outside the lab.

NEW: resizing, several monitors, zoom
  * Size: in Author mode every picture/clip has a Size row (S / M / L / XL + slider).
    Large sizes automatically move the picture below the text at full width.
    The Find-it panel can be resized by dragging its left edge.
  * Record clip -> "Add screen / window" once per monitor (up to 4). They record
    together and play back as one wide picture (choose side-by-side, stacked or 2x2).
    One monitor showing everything? One "Entire screen" is enough.
  * In the clip editor: Zoom tool (click where to zoom to), "Zoom out here",
    and "Auto-zoom to clicks" (zooms to each Click highlight, then back out).
    Viewers can switch zoom off with the magnifier button on the clip.
